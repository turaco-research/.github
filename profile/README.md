# Turaco Research

**Every language. Every device. Everyone.**

Turaco Research is a community-driven, open-source AI research initiative dedicated to developing lightweight and accessible artificial intelligence for underrepresented languages.

We believe that the language a person speaks, the device they own and the quality of their internet connection should not determine their ability to benefit from artificial intelligence.

## Why We Exist

Artificial intelligence is transforming how people communicate, learn, access information and interact with technology. Yet many of the world's languages remain inadequately represented in modern digital systems.

Even where language technologies exist, their computational requirements and dependence on cloud infrastructure can make them inaccessible to the communities that need them most.

Turaco Research exists to address both challenges: linguistic underrepresentation and computational inaccessibility.

Our mission is to bring more languages into the digital world through open research, community collaboration and efficient engineering.

## Our Three Pillars

### Linguistic Inclusion

Every language deserves meaningful digital representation.

We work towards supporting underrepresented languages, dialects and linguistic varieties while respecting their cultural context, regional differences and authentic usage.

### Open Source

We believe that scientific knowledge and technological progress should be accessible and collaborative.

We aim to openly share our models, datasets, methodologies, research findings and software wherever legally and ethically possible, enabling others to study, use, improve and build upon our work.

### Edge-First AI

Accessibility goes beyond making models freely available.

We prioritize lightweight, computationally efficient AI models designed to operate on everyday devices, including smartphones, standard computers and embedded systems.

Our goal is to minimize dependence on expensive hardware, continuous internet connectivity and cloud-based infrastructure without compromising practical linguistic quality.

## What We Aim to Build

| Research Area | Objective |
|---|---|
| Speech-to-Text | Enable machines to understand and transcribe underrepresented languages. |
| Text-to-Speech | Generate natural and intelligible speech in local languages. |
| Machine Translation | Bridge communication between languages. |
| Language Datasets | Build representative, validated and responsibly sourced linguistic resources. |
| Efficient AI | Research lightweight architectures, compression, quantization and knowledge distillation. |
| Edge Inference | Enable efficient local and offline model execution. |
| Developer Tools | Provide libraries, SDKs and integration resources that simplify adoption. |

Our research is not limited to a particular language, geographical region, model architecture or hardware platform.

## Our Approach

We bring together linguistic expertise, scientific research and practical engineering.

Our approach emphasizes:

- Community-driven data collection and linguistic validation.
- Responsible data governance and informed consent.
- Reproducible research and transparent experimentation.
- Efficient model architectures and hardware-aware development.
- Evaluation across linguistic quality and computational performance.
- Openly accessible models and supporting technologies.
- Practical deployment on affordable and widely available devices.

We measure success not only by what our models can do, but also by who can realistically use them.

## Get Involved

You do not need to be an AI researcher or software engineer to contribute.

We welcome:

- AI and machine learning researchers.
- Software, mobile and embedded systems engineers.
- Linguists and language researchers.
- Native and fluent speakers.
- Translators and transcribers.
- Data contributors and annotators.
- Students and educators.
- Universities and research institutions.
- Open-source contributors and volunteers.

Whether you contribute code, provide linguistic expertise, record speech, validate translations, test models or support research activities, your contribution can help make artificial intelligence more inclusive.

Visit our [Community Repository](https://github.com/turaco-research/community) and [Community Discussions](https://github.com/turaco-research/community/discussions) to get involved.
